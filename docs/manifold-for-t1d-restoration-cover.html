Manifold for T1D — Restoration

β-cell preservation, regeneration, and replacement-therapy analytics

Type 1 Diabetes does not end at diagnosis — three clinical paradigms are now converging on the restoration of β-cell function: preservation (teplizumab post-diagnosis, verapamil, ATG, GLP-1 combinations), regeneration (DYRK1A inhibitors, senolytics), and replacement (Vertex VX-880, Sernova Cell Pouch, ViaCyte/CRISPR). Each paradigm has actionable agents and rich longitudinal data; the analytic gap lies in matching patient to therapy and in ordering combination regimens on a principled causal graph. Manifold configures three engine capabilities to the domain — causal discovery on multi-omic and clinical-trial data, criticality estimators on post-intervention trajectories (mixed-effects C-peptide models, heterogeneous-treatment-effect learners, Gaussian process regression, Bayesian change-point), and Pearl do-calculus with interdiction for principled combination-therapy design.

Vertex VX-880's initial cohort achieving insulin-independence constitutes a proof-of-principle rather than a finished therapy. The outstanding question is no longer whether β-cell function can be restored, but for which patients, with which adjuncts, and over what duration — a problem of causal inference.

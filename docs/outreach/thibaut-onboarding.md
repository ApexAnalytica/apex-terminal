## How attribution works in practice

Before you make any introduction:

1. **Send the prospect's name and firm to Brynna** (brynna@apexanalytica.co). One line is enough: *"Considering reaching out to [Name] at [Firm] re: Manifold."*
2. Brynna confirms within 24 hours whether the prospect is already on our pipeline. If yes, attribution does not attach and we tell you so you can re-direct. If no, the prospect is logged as yours for a 12-month attribution window starting from your documented introduction.
3. Make the introduction. CC Brynna or me on the intro email so we have the documented timestamp.
4. From there, I handle the sales conversation, the configuration, the technical demo, the proposal, and the close. You stay in the loop and join calls where useful, but the front-line work is mine unless we agree otherwise.

This protects you from spending effort on a prospect we are already working, and it protects me from disputed attribution. Two minutes of Slack-and-confirm before every intro.

## What to do in the first 30 days

1. **Read the ICP doc.** Get specific about which of the three priority segments overlaps with your network.
2. **List your 10 strongest contacts** across those segments. Not 50. Not 100. The 10 where you would actually pick up the phone and call.
3. **Send me the list.** Indicates relationship strength (1 = barely know them, 5 = coffee tomorrow), current title, and one sentence on why they would care about a Manifold conversation.
4. **We sort the list together.** I tell you which one or two to pitch first, what version of the deck or 1-pager to send with the intro, and what specifically to emphasise based on what I know about that buyer profile.
5. **You make the first one or two intros.** We see what comes back. We tune from there.

I would rather have you make 2 high-quality intros in the first month than 20 generic ones. The 12-month attribution window means a single strong intro that closes in month 6 is worth more than a year of cold outreach.

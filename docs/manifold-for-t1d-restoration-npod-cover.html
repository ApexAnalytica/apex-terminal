Manifold for T1D — nPOD Tissue-Biology Integration

Pancreatic-tissue multi-omic causal inference across T1D disease stages

nPOD is the only resource producing pancreatic tissue across the full T1D disease spectrum — at-risk, new-onset, established, long-duration — paired with donor clinical history and cross-modality multi-omic characterization (histology, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, proteomics). The analytic frontier is structural: tissue-level causal inference across disease-stage strata, cross-modality integration onto a single directed graph, and a standardized fragility layer for cross-investigator comparison. Manifold configures causal discovery on the nPOD multi-modal substrate, criticality estimators (persistent homology on tissue manifolds, HTE learners, change-point across strata, spatial-transcriptomic coupling) feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF), and Pearl do-calculus with interdiction for tissue-level target prioritization.

What drives progression at the tissue level — from autoantibody positivity to clinical T1D to established hypoinsulinemia — is no longer a descriptive problem but a causal-inference problem on the integrated nPOD substrate.

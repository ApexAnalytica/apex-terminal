Manifold for Type 1 Diabetes

Research-partnership brief — pre-clinical risk stratification and intervention-window detection

1 · The clinical problem

Type 1 Diabetes has a years-long pre-clinical prodrome:

  • Stage 1 — ≥2 persistent islet autoantibodies, normoglycemia.
  • Stage 2 — dysglycemia (abnormal OGTT), still asymptomatic.
  • Stage 3 — clinical onset (~100% 10-year risk conditional on Stage 1).

Current risk-stratification tools (TrialNet DPTRS, Index60, M120) deliver strong population-level survival curves but fail to identify individual-trajectory inflection points — the window within which a Stage-2-appropriate intervention (teplizumab-class anti-CD3, low-dose ATG, experimental antigen-specific therapies) delivers maximal benefit.

Three capabilities remain underdeveloped in current practice:

  • Treatment of autoantibody seroconversion as a richer signal than a binary count.
  • Incorporation of continuous glycemic variability preceding overt dysglycemia.
  • Unified modeling of the causal structure between HLA haplotype, viral exposure (enterovirus / Coxsackie B), gut microbiome, and metabolic load, rather than parallel correlation studies.
Teplizumab's 2022 approval transformed the pre-clinical window from academic interest to actionable intervention. Each additional month of accurate per-patient stratification extends the period within which a Stage-2-appropriate intervention remains beneficial.

2 · What Manifold brings

Manifold is a causal-intelligence platform for detecting structural fragility and regime transitions in complex multi-signal systems. Three engine capabilities form the basis of the T1D configuration:

  1. Causal discovery (Spirtes / PC algorithm) on multi-omic cohorts. Replaces ad hoc correlation practice in GWAS × microbiome × virome × metabolomic integration with a principled conditional-independence-based directed acyclic graph.
  2. Criticality estimators feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF). Individual estimators are configured to the domain's dynamics; the composite fragility score provides a stable integration layer across them.
  3. Pearl do-calculus and interdiction. Therapy selection is framed as a maximum-attenuation intervention on the inferred causal graph — directly parallel to the teplizumab-class "delay onset" trial design.

T1D pre-clinical progression is a staged Markov / survival process with autoreactive positive feedback. The criticality layer is configured accordingly:

  • Variance-based early-warning signals on rolling CGM windows once continuous glucose data is onboarded — feeds the Temporal pillar.
  • Persistent homology on longitudinal multi-omic point clouds for topological regime detection across integrated immune-metabolic trajectories — feeds the Interaction and Cascade pillars.

5 · Proposed initial engagement (Phase 1)

The scope of the proposed Phase 1 engagement is defined to produce a deliverable of demonstrable analytic value to the partnering investigator within a contained time and data footprint.

  • Dataset. Retrospective slice of the TEDDY cohort — approximately 8,000 children, 15 years of serial autoantibody, HLA, limited metabolomic, and environmental exposure data. TrialNet PTP serves as secondary validation.
  • Access path. NIDDK Central Repository Data Use Agreement, co-sponsored by a TEDDY-affiliated principal investigator.
  • Deliverable. Per-subject ΩF trajectory combining DNB, change-point, and deep-survival estimators, benchmarked against DPTRS on Stage-3 prediction at 1, 3, and 5-year horizons.
  • Success criteria. AUC lift of at least 0.05 over DPTRS on matched subjects; calibration improvement measured by Brier score and calibration-in-the-large; human-interpretable ΩF-pillar decomposition per subject.
  • Timeline. Ten to twelve weeks from data access.
  • Excluded from Phase 1 (reserved for the Phase 2 prospective arm): live CGM ingestion, gut microbiome sequencing, virome, and fecal metabolomics.

6 · Rationale and timing

  • Teplizumab has been approved. The pre-clinical window is clinically actionable; Stage-2 patients are candidates for intervention today.
  • The TEDDY cohort is mature. Data access pathways are established, and the surrounding analytic community is sophisticated and receptive to novel methodology.
  • Relevant funding mechanisms are active. Breakthrough T1D (formerly JDRF) Prediction & Prevention, the Helmsley Charitable Trust T1D program, NIH NIDDK SBIR/STTR, and the Breakthrough T1D Industry Discovery & Development Partnerships track all have active Requests for Proposals in this area.
  • The platform is production-ready. Manifold's causal, criticality, and interdiction engines are production-grade; configuration for the T1D domain represents bounded, well-scoped work.

7 · Proposed partnership

Partnership with a TEDDY-affiliated principal investigator (or equivalent cohort steward — TrialNet, DiPiS, BabyDiab) is sought for:

  1. Retrospective data access under the partnering institution's existing Data Use Agreement.
  2. Clinical co-authorship on the benchmark manuscript.
  3. Co-application to Breakthrough T1D, the Helmsley Charitable Trust, or NIH NIDDK for the Phase 2 prospective arm.

In exchange, Apex Analytica contributes the causal-inference engine, the ΩF framework, and the engineering team. Apex retains commercial rights to the platform; research intellectual property from the joint work is shared on standard academic–industry terms.

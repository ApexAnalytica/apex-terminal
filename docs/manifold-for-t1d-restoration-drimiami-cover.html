Manifold for T1D — DRI Long-Horizon Transplant Analytics

Islet-transplant registry causal integration and BioHub translational prioritization

The Diabetes Research Institute holds an asset no other institution can replicate: the longest continuous programmatic record of clinical islet transplantation in the world, paired with the active BioHub translational program. That record — donor quality, recipient HLA and autoantibody profile, immunosuppression regimen, longitudinal C-peptide AUC, and long-horizon graft outcome — is a substrate for causal inference at a scale and horizon unmatched elsewhere. Manifold configures three engine capabilities to the domain: causal discovery on the registry's covariate structure, criticality estimators (long-horizon survival, HTE learners, mixed-effects decay, change-point) feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF), and Pearl do-calculus with interdiction for principled regimen and BioHub-variant prioritization.

The DRI record demonstrates that islet replacement can produce insulin-independence. The outstanding question is which donor, recipient, regimen, and delivery-architecture combinations sustain the outcome over a decade — a problem of causal inference on a uniquely long-horizon dataset.

Proposed partnership

Partnership with a Diabetes Research Institute principal investigator at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine is sought for:

  1. Retrospective DRI islet-transplant registry and BioHub data access under the DRI's existing Data Use Agreement and IRB.
  2. Clinical co-authorship on the long-horizon graft-survival and regimen-interdiction benchmark manuscript (twelve to fourteen weeks from data access).
  3. Co-application to Breakthrough T1D Cures, Helmsley Restoring Insulin Production, or NIH NIDDK for the Phase 2 prospective arm.

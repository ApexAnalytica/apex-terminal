Manifold for T1D — VX-880 Post-Infusion Analytics

Stem-cell-derived islet-therapy graft-function hazard and response decomposition

VX-880's initial cohort achieving insulin-independence is a stage-change proof-of-principle for stem-cell-derived islet therapy. The next five years will be defined by analytic questions, not biological ones: who reaches durable engraftment, what precedes graft compromise, and which second-generation regimens and adjuncts (including the encapsulated VX-264 variant) most attenuate loss-of-function hazard. Each post-infusion subject contributes an exceptionally dense longitudinal record — C-peptide AUC, CGM, inflammatory panels, immunosuppression pharmacokinetics, autoantibody titers — whose joint dynamical structure is under-exploited by endpoint-by-endpoint biostatistics. Manifold configures three engine capabilities to the domain: causal discovery on multi-signal post-infusion data, criticality estimators (Gaussian-process regression, Bayesian change-point, variance-based early warning, HTE learners) feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF), and Pearl do-calculus with interdiction for principled second-generation regimen design.

VX-880's first patients reaching insulin-independence is a proof-of-principle, not a finished product. The outstanding question is no longer whether β-cell function can be restored, but for which patients, with which immunosuppression regimens and adjuncts, and over what duration — a problem of causal inference on small-n high-signal trajectories.

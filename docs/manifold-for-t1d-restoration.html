Manifold for Type 1 Diabetes — Restoration

Research-partnership brief — β-cell function preservation, regeneration, and replacement-therapy analytics

1 · The clinical problem

T1D does not end at diagnosis — it begins a decades-long contest for residual and restored β-cell function. Three clinical paradigms are now converging:

  • Preservation. Residual β-cell mass at diagnosis predicts long-term complications, glycemic stability, and insulin requirement. Current practice treats the honeymoon phase as luck; a handful of agents (teplizumab extension, low-dose ATG, verapamil, GLP-1 combinations) measurably slow C-peptide decay but response is heterogeneous.
  • Regeneration. Pharmacologic β-cell proliferation is no longer hypothetical — DYRK1A inhibitors (harmine-class), GLP-1 / DYRK1Ai combinations, and senolytics have moved into human studies. The readout is slow and multi-signal; classical trial statistics under-exploit the data.
  • Replacement. Stem-cell-derived islet therapies (Vertex VX-880, Sernova Cell Pouch, ViaCyte/CRISPR) have produced insulin-independence in early-phase patients. Each trial has small n and immense per-patient signal; graft-survival and immune-escape dynamics are the bottleneck.

What today's analytic practice does not do well:

  • Separate heterogeneous treatment effect (who responds to teplizumab vs. verapamil vs. combo) from noise at realistic sample sizes.
  • Treat post-infusion metabolic trajectories (C-peptide, CGM variability, inflammatory panels) as a joint dynamical system rather than parallel endpoints.
  • Rationalize combination-therapy design (β-cell proliferation + immune tolerance + metabolic support) on a causal graph rather than by empirical stacking.
Vertex VX-880's first cohort achieving insulin-independence is a proof-of-principle, not a finished product. The question is no longer "can β-cell function be restored?" — it is "for whom, with what adjuncts, and for how long?" That question is causal-inference-shaped.

2 · What Manifold brings

Manifold is a causal-intelligence platform for detecting structural fragility and regime transitions in complex multi-signal systems. Three engine capabilities anchor the restoration configuration:

  1. Causal discovery (Spirtes / PC algorithm) on multi-omic + clinical-trial data. Recovers the directed structure between genotype, autoantibody profile, drug exposure, and C-peptide / CGM response — the substrate for principled heterogeneous-treatment-effect inference.
  2. Criticality estimators feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF). Configured for post-intervention dynamics: honeymoon-phase hazard, graft-compromise early-warning, combo-therapy response separation.
  3. Pearl do-calculus / interdiction. Frame combination-therapy design as max-attenuation intervention on the inferred causal graph — rational ordering of β-cell-proliferative, immune-tolerogenic, and metabolic adjuncts instead of empirical stacking.

4 · Criticality estimator configuration

Restoration data is small-n, longitudinally rich, and heterogeneous-in-response. The criticality layer is configured accordingly:

EstimatorWhat it catchesPillar feed
Nonlinear mixed-effects C-peptide decay modelsPer-subject honeymoon-phase prognosis; subject-level deviation from cohort decay curveR, T
Heterogeneous-treatment-effect learners (Causal Forest, S / T / X-learners, BART)Who responds to which drug or combination, under realistic trial sample sizesI, C
Gaussian process regression on sparse post-infusion trajectoriesGraft-function estimation and uncertainty with n < 20 and irregular follow-upR, T
Bayesian online change-point (BOCPD) on CGM + inflammatory panelsGraft-compromise and honeymoon-end detection with calibrated uncertaintyJ, T

Complementary estimators available in the same layer for v2:

  • Transfer entropy between inflammatory cytokine panels and C-peptide / CGM signals — quantifies the immune-metabolic coupling (J) that drives graft rejection and regenerative failure.
  • Variance-based early-warning signals on high-frequency CGM windows in the first 30 days post-infusion — leading indicator of graft-compromise before mean-function drops.

5 · Minimum viable collaboration (v1)

Smallest slice that produces something a principal investigator or trial steward will take seriously.

  • Dataset option A — preservation. Retrospective teplizumab / ATG / verapamil trial data with serial C-peptide AUC, MMTT, autoantibody titers. Joslin T1D Medalist honeymoon-cohort as long-horizon validation.
  • Dataset option B — replacement. Published or collaborator-provided phase 1/2 islet-cell-therapy post-infusion trajectories (Vertex VX-880 cohort-level summaries or partner-site raw data; Sernova Cell Pouch clinical data).
  • Access path. Breakthrough T1D or NIDDK HIRN-sponsored DUA with a trial-adjacent PI or consortium.
  • Deliverable. Per-subject ΩF trajectory with mixed-effects + HTE + GP agreement, benchmarked against the trial's published biostatistical analysis. For preservation: 6 / 12 / 24-month C-peptide hazard. For replacement: graft-function hazard and combo-response classification.
  • Success criteria. HTE recovery on a held-out trial arm matching or exceeding the pre-specified biostatistical analysis; identification of at least one interpretable responder / non-responder subgroup decomposable along ΩF pillars.
  • Timeline. 12–14 weeks from data access.
  • Out of scope v1 (reserved for v2 / prospective arm): live CGM ingestion from open-loop pumps, bespoke immunologic assays, multi-site prospective design.

6 · Why this, why now

  • Replacement has crossed the proof-of-principle threshold. Insulin-independence in early-phase Vertex VX-880 patients is a stage-change for the field. The next five years are about generalization — who responds, with which adjuncts — which is an analytic problem before it is a clinical one.
  • Pharmacologic regeneration is clinically tractable. DYRK1A inhibitor programs and GLP-1 / DYRK1Ai combinations are in human studies; their readout is multi-signal and slow, exactly the regime where composite-fragility framing outperforms endpoint-by-endpoint analysis.
  • Preservation has approved agents. Teplizumab post-diagnosis, verapamil, low-dose ATG, and GLP-1 adjuncts are all actionable today; the analytic gap is matching patient to drug, not discovering new drugs.
  • Funding lines are open. Breakthrough T1D Cures program, Helmsley Charitable Trust "Restoring Insulin Production" stream, and NIH NIDDK Human Islet Research Network (HIRN) consortium all have active RFPs in this space.
  • The platform is ready. Manifold's causal, criticality, and interdiction engines are production-grade; configuring them for the restoration domain is bounded, well-scoped work.

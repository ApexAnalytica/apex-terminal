Manifold for T1D — UBC SC-β Design Analytics

Stem-cell-derived β-cell differentiation, maturation, and graft-performance inference

SC-β cells have crossed from protocol to clinical insulin-independence. What is not yet established at the analytic level required for next-generation program design is the directed causal structure connecting differentiation-protocol parameters, maturation-state biomarkers, and post-graft functional outcome. UBC's Life Sciences Institute — with lineage through the ViaCyte program — holds a preclinical-to-clinical translation substrate unmatched elsewhere. Manifold configures causal discovery on the differentiation-to-outcome record, criticality estimators (GP regression on protocol surfaces, HTE learners on gene-edit variants, change-point on maturation, cross-species translation) feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF), and Pearl do-calculus with interdiction for encapsulation and gene-edit variant prioritization.

The field's next five years will be defined by protocol-to-performance generalization and by next-generation architecture design — a causal-inference problem on the legacy translation record and the high-dimensional design space.

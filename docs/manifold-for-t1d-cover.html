Manifold for Type 1 Diabetes

Pre-clinical risk stratification and intervention-window detection

T1D has a years-long pre-clinical prodrome. Current risk tools (TrialNet DPTRS, Index60, M120) give strong population survival curves but miss individual-trajectory inflection points — the window where a Stage-2-appropriate intervention has maximal benefit. Manifold is a causal-intelligence platform configured for T1D to detect those inflections: causal discovery on multi-omic cohorts, criticality estimators (DNB, Bayesian change-point, deep survival) feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF), and Pearl do-calculus / interdiction for principled therapy-selection framing.

Teplizumab's 2022 approval made the pre-clinical window actionable, not academic. Every month of better per-patient stratification is intervention-window bought.

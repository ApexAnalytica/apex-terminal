Manifold for Type 1 Diabetes

Pre-clinical risk stratification and intervention-window detection

Type 1 Diabetes has a years-long pre-clinical prodrome. Current risk-stratification tools (TrialNet DPTRS, Index60, M120) produce strong population-level survival curves but fail to identify individual-trajectory inflection points — the window within which a Stage-2-appropriate intervention delivers maximal benefit. Manifold is a causal-intelligence platform, configured for the T1D domain to detect these inflections: causal discovery on multi-omic cohorts, criticality estimators (DNB, Bayesian change-point, deep survival) feeding a composite fragility score (ΩF), and Pearl do-calculus with interdiction for principled therapy-selection framing.

Teplizumab's 2022 approval transformed the pre-clinical window from academic interest to actionable intervention. Each additional month of accurate per-patient stratification extends the period within which a Stage-2-appropriate intervention remains beneficial.
